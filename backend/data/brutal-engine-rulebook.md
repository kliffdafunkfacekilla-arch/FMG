## PART I: SYSTEM PHILOSOPHY & CORE ENGINE LOOPS

The B.R.U.T.A.L. Engine is built upon the principle of **Ludonarrative Grounding**. Combat is not an abstract exchange of turns; it is a violent, high-stakes **"Desperate Negotiation"** of physics, panic, and physiological exhaustion under the reality-warping stress of the Drift.

### 1. The Narrative Tag Rule
In this engine, character statistics and environmental features are not mere labels—they function as **Source Code**. 
* **Mechanical Reality:** When a physical or narrative interaction is established by the fiction, matching tags bypass the **Resolution Pipeline** entirely.
* **The Efficiency Hack:** If an environmental hazard or a target carries the **Brittle** tag, any strike or force designated as **Brutal** shatters it automatically. This bypasses health checks, defense rolls, and saving throws entirely, conserving critical S-Die and F-Die resources.

### 2. The 3-Beat Pulse (Action Economy)
Because of the immense physiological stress of high-stress combat (**Aetheric Backlash**), every biological chassis is limited to exactly three distinct beats per combat round.

| Beat Type | Governing Resource | Mechanical and Narrative Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Move Beat** | None (Standard) | Shifting between tactical Zones, scaling vertical structures, swimming, or sprinting. |
| **2. Stamina Action** | Stamina (S-Die) | Physical weapon strikes, melee parries, grappling maneuvers, and physical athletic feats. |
| **3. Focus Action** | Focus (F-Die) | Channeling reality-warping Anomalies, technical or environmental interactions, and mental feats. |

### 3. The Universal Action Battery
To survive the lethal opening seconds of a firefight, all combatants utilize an **Active Battery**. This represents immediate, short-term combat adrenaline.
* Regardless of Kingdom, Sub-Type, or gear, every combatant enters a combat encounter with an Active Battery of exactly **10/10 Stamina (S-Die)** and **10/10 Focus (F-Die)** tokens.
* These tokens are spent to initiate actions and fuel passive guard stances.

### 4. Biological Capacity, the Reserve Pool, and Reserve Burn
While the Active Battery is a fixed starting point, your **Biological Capacity** represents your body's underlying endurance and mental processing depth.
* **Stamina Capacity:** Might + Reflexes + Finesse
* **Focus Capacity:** Knowledge + Awareness + Intuition

#### The Reserve Pool
The **Reserve Pool** represents your long-term "gas tank"—the biological energy remaining after accounting for the physiological burden of your equipment:
$$\text{Reserve Pool} = \text{Biological Capacity} - \text{Total Gear Tax}$$

#### The Reserve Burn
If your 10-token Active Battery is depleted mid-firefight, you may perform a **Reserve Burn**. 
* This allows you to burn points from your Reserve Pool on a **1-to-1 basis** to instantly restore Active Stamina or Focus tokens.
* **Note:** This process does not lower your Maximum Biological Capacity, but the points are permanently lost until a **Full Rest** is completed. Burning your reserves is a desperate physiological sacrifice.

## PART II: THE TACTICAL TIMELINE (INITIATIVE & TIMING)

The B.R.U.T.A.L. Engine discards cyclical initiative. Instead, turn order is a procedural byproduct of **Information Advantage** and **Execution Advantage**.

### Phase 1: The Information Step (Perceptive Declaration)
Turn order begins with the declaration of intent. Highly observant characters gain the ability to read the battlefield's "Source Code" before committing to actions.
* **Declaration Order:** The combatant with the **Lowest Perception** sub-stat must declare their intent first. The combatant with the **Highest Perception** declares last.
* **Source Code Observation:** Highly perceptive entities witness enemy intent (incoming charges, spells being channeled) and can reactively counter them. 
* **The Perception Threshold:** Characters with a Perception sub-stat of **14 or higher** automatically identify environmental Narrative Tags (like *Volatile*, *Conductive*, or *Unstable*) for free, bypassing the Focus Beat normally required for investigation.

### Phase 2: The Resolution Step (Execution Order)
Once all intents are locked in, actions resolve in order of physical speed.
* **Execution Order:** Actions resolve strictly from the **Highest Movement (Speed)** sub-stat to the lowest. 
* **Simultaneous Clashes:** If Speed values are tied, or if contested rolls result in an exact tie, combatants enter a **Clash** (see below).

### Derived Tactical Sub-Stats
Tactical sub-stats are calculated using specific **2:1 ratios** to ensure that a character's core attributes contribute meaningfully to tactical success.

* **Perception (The Tracker/Sniper):** `Awareness + Logic + Vitality` (Ratio: 2 Mind : 1 Body). Governs Declaration Order and tag identification.
* **Stealth & Camo (The Ghost):** `Knowledge + Charm + Finesse` (Ratio: 2 Mind : 1 Body). Used to contest enemy Perception and secure ambushes.
* **Movement & Speed (The Skirmisher):** `Reflexes + Might + Intuition` (Ratio: 2 Body : 1 Mind). Determines Execution Order in the timeline.
* **Balance & Sure-Footedness (The Bastion):** `Endurance + Fortitude + Willpower` (Ratio: 2 Body : 1 Mind). Resists forced movement and displacement.

## PART III: RESOLUTION & BREAKING DEADLOCKS

### 1. The Clash Matrix
When a contested d20 roll results in an exact tie, combatants enter a **Clash**, representing an exhausting physical or mental deadlock.
* **The Cost of Attrition:** Clashing is punishing. Both combatants must immediately spend **1 Focus and 1 Stamina** token for every round the deadlock continues.
* **The Resolution Process:** Combatants secretly choose one of the six Clash Tactics, reveal them simultaneously, and compare the choices on the Clash Matrix below:

| Tactic | Winner's Body Stat | Winner's Mind Stat | Winner's Delivery (Effect) | Loser's Vulnerability |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Press** | Might | Knowledge | Steps 1 space forward, overpowering the center. | Overcommits; suffers amplified counter-damage. |
| **Hold** | Endurance | Logic | Anchors in place; strike delivered from fixed stance. | *Arcane Note:* Spells detonate mid-way, dealing half damage to both and creating a Hazard. |
| **Maneuver** | Reflex | Intuition | Shifts 1 space (L/R); strikes from a flanking angle. | Attempts to side-step; moves directly into the hit. |
| **Trick** | Finesse | Awareness | Alters strike frequency; bypasses all blocks. | Bluff exposed; left **Stunned** by psychological shock. |
| **Feint** | Fortitude | Willpower | Bait & Switch: Instantly switches spaces with the loser. | **Staggered:** Steps out of stance; absorbs strike unprotected. |
| **Disengage** | Vitality | Charm | Delivers parting strike; leaps 1 space backward. | Caught flat-footed; impact throws them farther backward. |

### 2. Specialized Conflicts
* **Magic Duels:** When casters target each other simultaneously, the combatant utilizing the elemental **Bane** of their opponent rolls with **Advantage**. Resolving a duel adds **+1 Tick** to the Chaos Tracker.
* **Compound Casting:** Two casters may pool resources, or a single caster may weave a spell over two turns, combining the **Target/Effect** of one with the **Method of Delivery** of another. This high-stakes action requires **two successful rolls** to resolve.
* **Forced Movement:** Any action intended to physically displace an entity (shoves, pulls, or tips) is tested against the defender’s **Balance & Sure-Footedness** sub-stat.

### Step 7: The 6-Piece Arsenal and Gear Tax
Equipping weapons and defensive gear places a physical and cognitive burden on your biological systems. Your loadout is strictly limited to exactly 3 Physical items and 3 Mental items. Equipping gear deducts from your Capacity to create your **Reserve Pool**.

| Gear Tier | Item Weight Examples | Resource Tax | Armor Mod (Damage Reduction) |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Light** | Silks, Daggers\*, Wards | -1 Stamina or Focus | 1 |
| **Medium** | Chain, Hammers, Circlets | -2 Stamina or Focus | 2 |
| **Heavy** | Plate, Greatswords, Relics | -3 Stamina or Focus | 3 |

*\*Light Melee Weapons (like daggers) are a surgical exception and cost **0 Tax** to equip.*

#### The 50% Threshold Rule
If your total combined Gear Tax exceeds **50% of your Maximum Biological Capacity** (Stamina or Focus respectively), your chassis is considered overburdened.
* **The Penalty:** Normal combatants restore **2 tokens** to their Active Battery at the start of their turn. If you violate the 50% threshold, your adrenaline regeneration is reduced to **1 token** per turn, crippling your combat stamina.

#### The Shock of Loss
Loadouts are mechanically locked during the adrenaline rush of combat. If armor or mental warding is shattered, disarmed, or stripped mid-fight, **you do not regain the taxed Capacity points**. The physiological shock and sudden shift in center of gravity preserve the tax until a Full Rest is completed.

## PART V: THE TRAUMA PIPELINE (CONSEQUENCES)

Damage in the B.R.U.T.A.L. Engine represents a transition from simple impacts to permanent biological trauma. The system utilizes physical **Trauma Cards** to handle **Delayed Resolution** cleanly.

### 1. Injury Thresholds
When an attack penetrates your Armor Mod (Damage Reduction), the net damage is compared to the following thresholds:

| Damage Received | Injury Severity | Immediate Effect | Lingering Effect (The Come-Down Phase) |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1–2** | Bruise/Flinch | Direct HP or Composure loss. | No lingering trauma. |
| **3–6** | Minor Injury | Immediate **Adrenaline Shock**. | Record 1 Trauma Card; resolve after combat. |
| **6–10** | Major Injury | Immediate **Adrenaline Shock** + continuous **Bleed** (Physical) or **Trauma** (Mental) damage. | Record 1 Trauma Card; resolve after combat. |
| **11+** | Critical Injury | Immediate **Adrenaline Shock** + continuous **Bleed/Trauma**. Cannot self-treat. | Record 2 Trauma Cards; resolve after combat. |

### 2. Adrenaline Shock (The Immediate Penalty)
Any hit reaching the Minor threshold or higher instantly triggers **Adrenaline Shock**.
* **The Rule:** The target suffers **Disadvantage on their very next roll**, regardless of action type.
* **Physical Card UI:** Draw a card from the Trauma Deck and place it **face-down** on your dashboard. The card back boldly displays **ADRENALINE SHOCK** as a reminder. Once you roll your next action, discard the card to your "pending" slot.

### 3. The Come-Down Phase (The Reveal)
When combat ends, the adrenaline fades. Flip your pending face-down cards to reveal where the strikes landed:

#### Physical Trauma Cards (HP Damage)
* **Legs (Fractured Mobility):**
  * *Tier 1 (First Draw):* Movement distance is halved.
  * *Tier 2 (Second Draw - Escalation):* Disadvantage on all Active Defense rolls.
* **Arms (Shattered Output):**
  * *Tier 1:* Disadvantage on all physical Offense rolls.
  * *Tier 2 (Escalation):* Disadvantage on both Offense and Defense rolls.
* **Core (Internal Rupture):**
  * *Tier 1:* Movement is halved; gain the **Staggered** tag.
  * *Tier 2 (Escalation):* Disadvantage on all sustained/Clash actions.
* **Head (Concussive Force):**
  * *Tier 1:* Disadvantage on Perception checks and Information Advantage.
  * *Tier 2 (Escalation):* Disadvantage on all Attack and Defense rolls.

#### Mental Trauma Cards (Composure Damage)
* **Confidence (Ego Collapse):**
  * *Tier 1:* Disadvantage on Social Defense.
  * *Tier 2 (Escalation):* Permanently lose access to the *Self-Assured* and *Impressive* tags.
* **Reason (Logic Fracture):**
  * *Tier 1:* Disadvantage on Focus Actions and Spells.
  * *Tier 2 (Escalation):* Cannot use any Logic-based skills.
* **Instinct (Sensory Blindness):**
  * *Tier 1:* Disadvantage on Initiative/Intuition.
  * *Tier 2 (Escalation):* Cannot read enemy or ally intent (auto-fail Insight checks).
* **Memory (Identity Bleed):**
  * *Tier 1:* Disadvantage on Willpower and Lore checks.
  * *Tier 2 (Escalation):* Auto-fail saving throws against *Fear* or *Deceptive* tags.

### 4. The Zero-State (Systemic Failure)
At 0 HP (Neutralized) or 0 Composure (Psychic Break), an entity falls Prone and enters **The Descent**—a 3-round countdown to expiration.
* **Shock Wipe Rule:** Returning from the brink of death is violently traumatic. Any medical or psychiatric treatment that revives a character instantly wipes their Active Action Pools (Stamina and Focus) to exactly **0 tokens**.
* **Exposed Defenses:** Revived characters awaken with the **Winded** or **Confused** tag. Their defenses collapse to a flat **DC 10** until the end of the combat round.
* **Instant Expiry:** If a character reaches **-10 Capacity** through continuous Bleed or Trauma, they suffer instant biological or mental death.

## PART VI: THE CHAOS ENGINE & REALITY INSTABILITY

Using professional skills and reality-bending anomalies generates systemic friction within the Drift, tracked via the **Chaos Tracker**.

### 1. The Glitch Trigger
A Glitch occurs when a d20 roll matches the current **Chaos Number**. The glitch margin expands as the tracker clicks upward:
* **4–6 Ticks:** $\pm 1$ margin
* **7–9 Ticks:** $\pm 2$ margin
* **10 Ticks (Zone Envelopment):** Local reality shatters completely, overwriting physics (e.g., Gravity Flip or Hyper-Lethality).

### 2. Channeling Chaos
If a combatant has **0 active tokens**, they may choose to **Channel the Chaos**.
* Roll a standard d20 alongside a **Chaos Die** (d20).
* If the Chaos Die matches the current Chaos Number, the action's effectiveness is doubled.
* If the Chaos Die misses the Chaos Number by **5 or more**, the Drift violently rejects the conduit: the action fails and triggers a **Wild Resonance** targeting *only the self*.

### 3. Wild Resonance Chart Excerpts (1-20)
1. **Kinetic Reversal:** Attacker gains Disadvantage on their next roll; target gains Advantage on their next roll.
2. **Elemental Swap:** Target is unharmed; target's space permanently gains the **Corrosive** or **Hot** tag.
3. **Gravitational Slingshot:** Instantly swap the physical miniature locations of the attacker and the target.
4. **Vocal Echo:** Target is unharmed; the entire Zone permanently gains the **Mute** tag.
5. **Sensory Swap:** The room's layout physically rotates 90 degrees underneath all miniatures.
6. **Temporal Stutter:** Target instantly gains a free Move beat.
7. **Dimensional Phase:** Target's muscles lock; they lose their next Stamina (S-Die) action beat.
8. **Friction Loss:** Attacker slides 2 Zones away; ground beneath them becomes **Unstable**.
9. **Psychic Backlash:** Converted to Composure damage; target gains the **Confused** tag.
10. **Blood to Acid:** Target is unharmed; floor beneath them gains the **Corrosive** tag.
20. **The Perfect Storm:** All entities in the Zone instantly fall **Prone** and gain the **Exposed** tag. Add **+3 Ticks** to the Chaos Tracker.
