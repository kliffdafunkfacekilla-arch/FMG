# The Twelve Elemental Conductors — The Great Wheel (revised — combat-order version)
A complete, closed 12-step cycle. Two versions of this loop existed in the source; this is the confirmed final order — a tactical Weakness/Immunity chain rather than an industrial absorb/release chain, and the more intuitive of the two (fire burns arcane secrets, decay conquers life, poison corrupts spirit, ice melts under light). Each element's Power is **destroyed by** the element before it and **immune to** the element after it:

| Element | Power / Dragon | Destroyed By (Weakness) | Immune To (Shield) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead | Motus (Vecelo) | Aura (Phosphorus) — Spirit/Charm | Lux (Chromium) — Radiant/Light |
| Chromium | Lux (Opecten) | Motus (Lead) — Sonic/Velocity | Ordo (Titanium) — Cold/Stasis |
| Titanium | Ordo (Stagus) | Lux (Chromium) — Radiant/Light | Lex (Lithium) — Psychic/Willpower |
| Lithium | Lex (Tyrustis) | Ordo (Titanium) — Cold/Stasis | Flux (Gold) — Acid/Reaction |
| Gold | Flux (Aurgenas) | Lex (Lithium) — Psychic/Willpower | Ratio (Silicon) — Shock/Electricity |
| Silicon | Ratio (Metrion) | Flux (Gold) — Acid/Liquidity | Nexus (Tungsten) — Fire/Heat |
| Tungsten | Nexus (Carulkem) | Ratio (Silicon) — Shock/Logic | Anumis (Bismuth) — Arcane/Secrets |
| Bismuth | Anumis (Lophex) | Nexus (Tungsten) — Fire/Heat | Mass (Osmium) — Force/Gravity |
| Osmium | Mass (Tiraton) | Anumis (Bismuth) — Arcane/Secrets | Omen (Iridium) — Necrotic/Time |
| Iridium | Omen (Termhill) | Mass (Osmium) — Force/Gravity | Vita (Silver) — Poison/Mutation |
| Silver | Vita (Gavusrix) | Omen (Iridium) — Necrotic/Decay | Aura (Phosphorus) — Spirit/Charm |
| Phosphorus | Aura (Virantor) | Vita (Silver) — Poison/Mutation | Motus (Lead) — Sonic/Velocity |

The inherent risk of any sigil-paste conduit failing is the **Blowout** (Section 5) — localized gravity flips, instant biological petrification, or living flesh transforming into inanimate metal.

While actively channeling one of the Twelve Powers, a wielder becomes a living node of this same wheel — the **Ouroboros Loop**. This grants total immunity to the element that follows them, but a catastrophic, disproportionate vulnerability to the element that precedes them. This is the exploited weakness behind **the Mandate of the Chain** (Section 4.20) — a militant anti-magic order of Nulls (biologically immune to the psychic feedback of the weave) who capture exhausted wielders and execute them with chains forged from their exact **Breaker Metal**, using the wielder's own power against them in the most literal sense.



A newly added source (a structured character-bible file) confirms all twelve Magistars were originally **mortal rulers of a pre-Shattering political order called the Dominion**, each transformed by "The Shattering" rather than born a dragon. Every entry matched the existing roster cleanly — only Gavusrix's title needed correcting (above). Worth treating as canon; consistent, well-sourced, and it gives every dragon a real personal downfall story rather than just a domain and an epithet:

Each dragon's corruption effect reads as a twisted, magnified version of their mortal title/hubris (e.g., Metrion "The Architect" becomes "The Crystalline Mind," logic taken past the point of sanity). Sh'lar is the exception — she was a Fae Advisor, not a Dominion ruler, and chose to dissolve into the chaos willingly rather than being transformed against her will.



- **"Sorel" (corrected):** not a rejected epithet after all — a later, more authoritative character-bible source confirms Sorel is Gavusrix's **pre-corruption mortal name** (who she was before "The Shattering" transformed her), not a competing title. Her actual Dragon Form epithet is **"The Quicksilver Mother"** — that source is more reliable here than my earlier guess of "The Fecund Plague," which is retired instead. "The Fecund Plague" can still exist as regional slang the Sump-Kin/Toad Barons use informally, just not her formal title.
- **"Kael, The Iron Hide"** — retired as an alt-name for Carulkem. All of Kael's material (Forgespire, "Implacable Siege," Tungsten) is just Carulkem's own lore restated; folded straight in under **The Furnace Eternal**.
- **Igni (The Raging Maw)** — retired as a standalone dragon. Igni's signature effects — firestorms and flash-freeze — are exactly Tungsten's conductor behavior (stops Fire, flash-freezes via Cold). That's Carulkem's element. Igni is now the Scute Confederacy's regional name for **Carulkem**; the Scute just don't call him "The Furnace Eternal." Their lore (Divination Pit, Black Scale 7, Storm-Lancer Corps) is untouched — only the dragon they're standing next to changes label.
- **Korus (The Hollow King)** — retired. His one mention (the Eastern Hounds naming the Past "a weapon used by the Hollow King to drive them mad") is just the Eastern Hounds' own name for **Termhill**, whose Omen/temporal-bleed domain is already exactly what's causing the Hounds' Haunting and their Grave-Berry amnesia culture. Folded in as a regional epithet, not a separate dragon.
- **Lophex** — kept, and un-orphaned. He was previously getting tagged as "Ratio" (Metrion's domain) in one place and drifting toward "Ordo" (Stagus's domain) in another — neither of which he needs, since Bismuth/Arcane-Nullification/Null-Equation is distinctive enough to stand on its own. He now owns **Anumis** outright, the one slot that had no patron. His faction ties (Null-Equation cult, the Concord's "Crystalline Void" zone) are unchanged — only his home domain is now fixed.
