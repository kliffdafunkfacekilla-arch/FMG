# 2. The Twelve Magistars (Chaos Dragons) and Their Domains
Twelve mortal rulers of the Dominion, one per Power, one per Sacred Grove — transformed into dragons via the mass-sacrifice ascension ritual described in Section 1, not Fae Elders themselves. No 13th-that-isn't-Sh'lar, no duplicate claims on a domain. Where old drafts named a dragon without tying it to one of these twelve, that lore is folded into the dragon whose domain/element it actually matches.

Plus one deliberate outlier, kept separate from the twelve on purpose:

- **Sh'lar, the 13th Shadow** — not a Grove-bound dragon. The "Prime Corruptor," a non-corporeal influence that acts as puppet-master/"Anti-Warden" to the Worm Cults. Stays outside the Power system entirely — that's the point of calling it the 13th.

**On Ratio specifically:** it's not just "math and logic" — Ratio is the Power that imposes *pattern* on things. Logic/calculation is a downstream expression of that (the mind finding the pattern), but the domain itself is broader: repetition, structure, predictability, the underlying shape that makes a system legible. This is what makes Metrion's patronage of the Meridian Chain make sense — a cartel whose entire business is reading and imposing patterns on chaos (Phase-Charts, the Blue-Route, the Ghost-Light Network's stable corridors) rather than raw calculation for its own sake.
