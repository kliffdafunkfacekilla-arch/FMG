# The Twelve Powers — Manifestation & Cost
Confirmed consistent across a dedicated "Powers" source, with rich sensory detail for how each feels to use. Worth keeping as a reference table:

| Power | Wielder Effect | Sensory Tell |
|---|---|---|
| Mass | Crushing gravity, gravity-well deflection | Subsonic grinding, oppressive air pressure, objects roll toward the wielder |
| Nexus | Primordial forge-heat, incineration, heat-absorption | Furnace-blast heat, scorched-iron smell, glowing veins/eyes when angered |
| Ordo | Stasis, absolute cold, entropy-rejection | Sudden silence and cold, breath crystallizes, wielder is icy and unnervingly still |
| Lex | Reality-obeying spiritual mandates | Crushing pre-thunderclap air pressure, resonant multi-layered voice, perfect unnatural posture |
| Motus | Extreme acceleration/deceleration, frictionless movement | Deafening sonic crack, wielder vibrates/taps constantly, speaks too fast to follow |
| Flux | Alchemical unbinding, matter melts/dissolves | Sulfur/acid stench, air shimmers like heat-warp, boneless liquid movement |
| Vita | Rampant, uncontrolled biological growth | *(consistent with Gavusrix's Fecund domain — describes the power itself, not a competing title for her)* |
| Ratio | Reality snapping back into balance — chain-lightning, "glitching" attacks | Burnt ozone smell, static crackle, wielder is twitchy and permanently frizz-haired |
| Lux | Blinding truth-revealing light, invisibility | Ringing silence, shadows erased by brilliance, translucent skin, masters cast no shadow at all |
| Omen | Accelerated decay/entropy, phase-stepping out of sync with the present | Dust/old-tomb smell, sudden sorrow, shadow lags behind movement, clocks stop nearby |
| Aura | Weaponized emotion — despair, addiction, obsessive love | Scent of nostalgic memory, nearby hearts race or drop, wielder has captivating dilated eyes |
| Anumis | Unmaking the structural "truth" of an object/pattern | Heat-mirage ripple, sickening hum, wielder's eyes dart reading invisible text, ink-stained fingers |
