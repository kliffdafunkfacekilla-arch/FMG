**Why the cults work this way — "Starving the Gods":** a Sacred Grove can't be broken by outside force. The only way to break a seal is to stop the chaos flow feeding it, lowering the pressure long enough for the dragon to physically overpower the weakened containment. So cultists don't attack the prisons directly — they heavily attune themselves to their patron's specific power and gather upstream in massive numbers, acting as living sponges that willingly suffer horrific mutation to siphon the flow away before it reaches the Grove. They're starving the prison by drinking the poison themselves.

**The real stakes — "The Domino Effect":** the Order of the Clockwork's greatest fear isn't one dragon escaping — it's what happens next. A freed Magistar instantly becomes a walking vacuum for primordial chaos, absorbing ambient flow at an exponential rate and forcibly diverting the leyline streams meant for the other eleven prisons. Those prisons would starve in turn, the rest would break free in a cascade, and the shattered continents — no longer held together by the Ley-line Lattice — would drift apart into the void. This is why the Order treats even a single successful cult as an extinction-level threat, not a regional problem.



Both tables agree Gavusrix's element is **Silver**, matching what we already locked in.
