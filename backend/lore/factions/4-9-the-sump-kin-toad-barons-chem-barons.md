# 4.9 The Sump-Kin (Toad Barons / Chem-Barons)

**Patron Dragon:** Gavusrix, The Quicksilver Mother (Vita) — matches the "Quicksilver Mother" cult entry directly ("Toad Barons farming Gavusrix to release the Fecund Plague" — that's the effect they're chasing, not her title).

**Role:** The Sump-Kin process raw materials and toxic industrial sludge for everyone else — they're the mechanism behind the "Geopolitical Hypocrisy" that both the Coastal Theocracy and the Heartland Alliance rely on to keep their own hands clean (buying refined goods through River Folk middlemen while the actual toxic processing happens in the Sump). They believe they can "farm the Dragon" — pumping sludge directly into Gavusrix's prison ecosystem to harvest mutated biological resources. Given her domain is Vitality/biomancy, this is a genuinely dangerous game: cultivating monstrous abundance right next to a chaos dragon whose whole thing is uncontrolled life.

**Shadow Week — the Lantern Chain:** River Folk families gather on their toxic rivers and float thousands of lanterns across their domain, staying awake all seven-plus nights to tend them. It's not just symbolic — a gap in the lantern chain is an immediate, practical signal that an upstream watch-post has failed, a real early-warning system built from their culture's deep-seated belief in mutual reliance.

**The Order of Purifiers ("The White-Robed Fanatics"):** a secret religious sect within River Folk society, composed exclusively of albino-born individuals taken from their families at birth. Socially paradoxical — unspoken of, their presence deeply uncomfortable to others, yet treated with such reverence that failing to offer them tribute is a grave social misstep for any successful trader. Specters within their own society: seen, but never acknowledged.
