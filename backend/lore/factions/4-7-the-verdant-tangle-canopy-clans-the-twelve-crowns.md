# 4.7 The Verdant Tangle (Canopy Clans) — The Twelve Crowns

**Patron Dragon:** Aurgenas, The Gilded Solvent (Flux) — consistent.

**Core conceit:** "Value rots if it sits. Keep it moving." Aurgenas's Flux-Rot curse makes stagnation literally corrosive — currency, goods, and structures decay if left stationary, so Tangle society runs on obsessive circulation of wealth (the **Twelve Crowns**, a meritocracy ranked by spending velocity, not hoarding). **Gold** is used industrially as insulation (stops Acid, per the elemental conductor chain) to keep Aether-Tech from being dissolved by the Patron's own rot.

**Foreign relations:** the Tangle's shifting, biologically hostile terrain corrodes the Iron Caldera's rigid steel machinery within hours, forcing the Ironborn into a dependency relationship — Sciurini couriers and Ailurus-provided coolants in exchange for access. Avians pay steep "Guide Fees" to navigate the Tangle's ever-shifting geography.

**The Sap-Sworn ("The Drunken Druids"):** the Tangle's most beloved and feared internal faction — spiritual guides, ecological regulators, and de facto park rangers, based in the Ferment-Halls (massive hollowed burls on the **Mother-Tree**, reeking of yeast, honey, and ozone). They don't cast from books; they drink their magic, believing the jungle itself has a consciousness only reachable by lowering mental barriers through sacred intoxication (**"the Tune"**). Structured in two phases — the Pathfinders (Rangers) and the Circle-Keepers (the Attuned).
