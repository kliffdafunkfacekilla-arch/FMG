# 4.17 The Guerrilla Clans (The Unseen Hand)

**Patron Dragon:** none found anywhere in the source — consistent with your point that not every faction needs one. Their identity is entirely self-made: small folk (feline, rabbit, and rodent kin, organized into Five specialized Clans — the Iron-Briars, Crescent-Wings, Crimson Carapace, Honorable Shadows, and Knot-Cloak Dragoons) who got tired of being trampled by larger armies and retreated into "the deep shadows" to build a culture around disappearance rather than conquest.

**Territory:** Subterranean Canopy Vaults, Deep Shadow Ridges, and Camouflaged Valleys — reclusive, deliberately hard to find. Likely the Sylvan Empire's western neighbor referenced earlier.

**Doctrine — "The Unseen Hand":** asymmetrical warfare built on patient observation rather than force: study an adversary's patterns and weaknesses before ever engaging. When the Ghostwind Raiders' stolen skiff violated Clan airspace, the Clans didn't kill the trespassers — they disabled the ship, brought it down, and let the encounter play out into the Raiders' origin story instead. A "No-Fly Zone" over their territory is respected by even the Free Sky-Baronies, who learned the hard way not to test it.

**Technology — "The Art of the Silent Sigil":** deliberately rejects the loud, volatile Aetheric-Reaction Engine technology everyone else uses, in favor of single-use charms painted by hedge-wizards — Leap-Charms (a silent 50-foot jump), Pop-Bolts (a non-lethal concussive burst for stunning sentries), and similar disposable tools built for silence over firepower.

**Economy:** A decentralized, low-tech Charm Economy running on barter and Trade-Charms rather than currency — practical goods (bread, linen, wine, tools) instead of gold, since a small, stealth-focused, mobile culture has no use for hoarded wealth or heavy industrial resources.
