# 4.10 The Ghostwind Raiders

Not a nation — a **deniable black-ops fleet**, classified internally as "Ghost-Link" assets. Publicly, they're a pirate consortium; in reality, they're a single ship (not a fleet at all — the "phantom fleet" is a myth) crewed by a cell of **the Guerrilla Clans** (small cat, rabbit, and rodent folk), and jointly funded off the books by the **Avian Empire's Gilded Compass** (for plausible deniability while disrupting rival trade) and the **Meridian Chain Hexarchy**, orchestrated under the covert oversight of a named handler, **Grand-Link Nero** — funding Black Ops and asset denial while maintaining a public face of "Mutual Profit." Both of the Chain's respectable partners are quietly running the same deniable proxy through them — worth keeping as an intentional irony rather than "fixing." Their mandate distills into three tactical priorities: **Surgical Extraction** (seizing Aether-Tech/Dragonstone from Chaos-Free Corridors before rivals can), **Systemic Sabotage** (destroying rival infrastructure in Bridge-Cities, disguised as natural Meander shifts), and **Groves Reconnaissance** (mapping imprisoned Magistars' Signature Chaos for the Meridian Chain).
