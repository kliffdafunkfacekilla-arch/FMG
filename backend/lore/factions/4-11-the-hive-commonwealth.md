# 4.11 The Hive Commonwealth

Defined by a literal telepathic **Hive Mind** — a collective psychic architecture that functions as a "Grounded Circuit," anchoring its members against the ambient chaos of the Abyssal Frontier. Occupies the **Quartz-Vein Corridor**, a high-psychic-resonance zone. Their own patron/target relationship is the first line of the 13 Cults table: Tyrustis (Lithium) — the same dragon whose domain (Lex/Willpower) makes psychic-terrorism against a hive-mind thematically sharp, since Lithium is specifically the conductor that stops Psychic effects.
