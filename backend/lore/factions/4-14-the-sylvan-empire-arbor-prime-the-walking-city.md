# 4.14 The Sylvan Empire — Arbor-Prime, the Walking City

**Territory:** "The Emerald Scar" (also "The Moving Forest"). **Aliases:** The Living Bastion, The Slow Council, The Wood-Wrights.

**Patron Dragon:** none, fixed. *(Correcting my own earlier proposal — Lophex already has a confirmed home: the Flower Valley Folk, see below. The real answer turned out better anyway: "The Schism of the Scar" is the shared origin story for both Sylvan Empire and the Flower Valley Folk. Both descend from one united botanical civilization that split when Vecelo's sonic chaos intensified — the Sylvan Elders chose to uproot and keep moving forever rather than fight or settle, while a splinter group went to root themselves at the Engineer's Legs instead. Because Sylvan Empire never stopped migrating, they never settled near a new Grove to inherit a patron from — which is exactly why their territory picks up diffuse secondary bleed-over from whichever flow zones they're currently passing through (Stagus/Ordo and Tiraton/Mass to the south, per their current position) rather than one fixed dragon of their own. Vecelo/Motus remains their defining historical trauma, just not their patron — Heartland Alliance keeps him uncontested.)*

*(Minor internal inconsistency in the source, not resolved: one document gives their core philosophy as "Speed is a brittle cage. We do not run, for to run is to be chased. We stand, and the storm breaks against us." — which reads as a stand-and-endure philosophy, directly at odds with the eternal-migration identity confirmed everywhere else, including in the same document. Treating the migration as canon since it's corroborated far more heavily; flagging the quote as likely a draft error rather than rewriting it into something it isn't.)*

**Founding myth:** Arbor-Prime is not built, it migrates — a collection of the Empire's greatest **Ancients** (living, ambulatory trees/beings), drifting at a glacial pace to outrun **Vecelo's Silent Echo**. This is preserved as *historical* trauma — an old threat from before their relocation to their current territory, not their present-day patron (Vecelo belongs to Heartland Alliance). *(Corrected: the founding text describes "the bedrock rotating... against the pressure of the void" — old cosmology language. Under the new model, it's not the ground rotating; it's the leyline flow shifting around their migration path, which is exactly what forces them to keep moving rather than settle.)*

**Industry — Dendro-Engineering:** Where the Ironborn and Canopy Clans believe "to create is to destroy" (melting ore, felling timber), the Sylvans grow their tools with intent — living Iron-Wood and sap-vein constructs that get *stronger* with age rather than rusting or decaying, directly subverting Aurgenas's Flux-Rot curse through biological rather than mechanical means.
