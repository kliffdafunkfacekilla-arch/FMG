# 4.21 The Free Sky-Baronies (The Outlaw Syndicate)

**Patron Dragon:** none named directly — fits the pattern of factions that don't need one. Interesting structural note: the Syndicate is a federation of **12 Barons**, each controlling a "Chapter"/Turf built around a trade route near a specific Sacred Grove — meaning each Chapter likely has its own regional dragon-flavor without the Syndicate as a whole answering to any single patron.

**Identity:** Explicitly *not* a "Robin Hood" faction — a gritty, blue-collar **Outlaw Motorcycle Club** structure. Union haulers who turned to organized crime to survive rather than knights-errant. Doctrine: **"The Sovereignty of the Skiff"** — reject all "Dry-Land Law" (Avian, Vaneer, Scute), on the belief that the sky cannot be owned by anyone who only wants to tax it.

**Territory:** Not fixed settlements — **Tethered Free-Yards** and **Drift-Slums**, massive lashed-together flotillas hidden deep in high-altitude Aetheric drift currents. Their operational doctrine is codenamed **the "Ghost Flotilla" strategy** — not a trackable standing fleet, but a network of hidden, temporary, camouflaged bases deliberately kept away from any controlled port, making them nearly impossible to pin down or engage conventionally.

**Initiation — "Taking the Burn":** a life-and-hull commitment, not a symbolic patch — a physical, bio-mechanical branding rite (**the Core-Brand**) rather than a sewn cloth patch like a typical MC.

**Origin:** "The Hauler's Rebellion." **Economy:** "Liquid Gold" — functionally the Prohibition Kings of the Shatterlands, running a monopoly-and-laundering operation. *(Likely the group referenced elsewhere as the Order of the Clockwork's third Triple-Anchor power, alongside the Gilded Compass and the Ivory-Gate Syndicate — distinct from Ivory-Gate despite both being sky-based toll/mercenary powers; worth keeping them clearly separate given how similar their niches are.)*
