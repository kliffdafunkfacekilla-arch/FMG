# 4.20 The Mandate of the Chain (also known as the Covenant of Null)

**Patron Dragon:** none — a militant anti-magic theocracy, and their whole identity is built on rejecting the Spark entirely, so no patron fits by design.

**Two names, one movement:** rooted in the biological reality that the Spark is a fluke, not a birthright — roughly half of Ostraka's population (the Null) has zero magical ability. Depending on the region and how organized a given cell is, this movement goes by either name: "Covenant of Null" emphasizes its grassroots, populist, anti-Spark-elite identity politics; "Mandate of the Chain" emphasizes its more structured theocratic institutions (below). Both describe the same underlying faction, not two separate groups. Like the Order of the Clockwork, they function as a check in the Triple-Hustle — preventing Spark-based elites from consolidating a monopoly on power.

**Origin — a holy text born from a misfiled memo:** centuries ago, wandering tribes unearthed a fragmented backup cache of **Warden lore** — a technical manual on Aetheric grounding vectors — and, lacking any context, mistranslated it into scripture: **The Way of the Uncursed**. To the Mandate, being born Null isn't a biological fluke, it's a state of divine purity, and magic itself is a pollution to be chained and bled out of the world.

**Tools — the Null-Chain:** their signature weapon, woven from Voltaic Fleece (Section 4.4's Sky-Grazer wool), functions as a kinetic sponge lethal specifically to Spark-users — the everyday, grassroots-level version of the same grounding principle their theocratic Rite of the First Link formalizes below.

**Recruitment — the Rite of the First Link:** recruits exclusively from the Null population (who make up the bulk of Ostraka's oppressed underclass, per Section 2's demographic table). New members grip a saturated iron chain link; if they secretly carry even a faint Spark, the resulting feedback — a **Spark-Bias Blowout** — instantly petrifies them or unravels their mind into "senseless math and crystalline lattice." The Mandate treats this horrific death as the chain purifying a liar, not a tragedy. This is a direct, brutal application of the **Ouroboros Vulnerability** (Section 2) — using a wielder's own Breaker Metal against them.

**Hierarchy — the Twelve Chains:** rank is earned by physically hauling saturated Breaker Metals (Lead, Tungsten, Osmium, and so on) to ground out each of the Twelve Powers in turn. To become Grand Leader, a zealot who's earned all twelve chains must survive a pilgrimage into the **Convergence** itself — the permanent chaos storm at the center of the Shattering — and return with a thirteenth chain forged from raw Dragonstone. *(Corrected: the source describes this Dragonstone as "a crystallized fragment of the Celestial Clockwork" — that's the old, wrong origin story. Per Section 5, it's meteoric debris from Erranith's periodic close passes; the Mandate's thirteenth chain is forged from a piece of that, not a Clockwork fragment.)*

**Irony, per the Archivist's own notes:** whoever compiled this material (in-universe, credited to "Clary") flags that the Mandate misunderstood the Warden archive they built their entire religion on — and that their zealotry accidentally *recharges the Grove seals* rather than threatening them. A militant order dedicated to destroying magic is, functionally, unknowingly helping maintain the Patch.
