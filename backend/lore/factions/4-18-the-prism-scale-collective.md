# 4.18 The Prism-Scale Collective

**Patron Dragon:** none found — a purely cultural/psychological power structure, consistent with the pattern of factions that don't need one.

**Core psychology — the obsession with Attention and Light:** a lizard-folk society (Crystal Lizards, Komodo Guards among them) where being seen, heard, and admired isn't a social benefit, it's the primary measure of a person's worth. Status comes from captivating an audience, not wealth or territory — every citizen is effectively a performer competing in a zero-sum game for the spotlight. To be ignored, uninteresting, or "dull" is the ultimate social sin.

**The Four Harmonic Forms:** not a monolithic species but four distinct biological castes, each filling a specialized, interdependent social role — the Anoles (Virtuosos), the Chameleons (Canvases), the Geckos (Climbers), and the Komodo Dragons (Bass Line).

**Justice — "Being Dull":** the Collective's real underbelly. Punishment for failing to shine isn't execution or imprisonment — it's the total, systematic erasure of identity. The offender is broken down until nothing of their former self remains, then re-emerges as a scarred, unwaveringly loyal bodyguard assigned to protect the noble houses. A closed-loop system of social engineering: it removes non-conformists and manufactures elite enforcers from the same process, with zero waste. Every dazzling performance is silently shadowed by these broken, loyal figures — a living reminder of what happens to anyone who stops shining.
