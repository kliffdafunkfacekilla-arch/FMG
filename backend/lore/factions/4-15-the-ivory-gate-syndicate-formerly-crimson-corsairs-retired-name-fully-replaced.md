# 4.15 The Ivory-Gate Syndicate (formerly "Crimson Corsairs" — retired name, fully replaced)

**Retcon note:** "Crimson Corsairs" is cut from canon entirely. Every reference to them — including their role in the Order of the Clockwork's Triple-Anchor Strategy (alongside the Gilded Compass and Free Sky-Barons) and Mama Koda's origin story (she worked smuggling muscle on their barge before becoming the Pirate Queen) — now reads as the Ivory-Gate Syndicate instead. No separate entity ever existed; this is a rename, not two factions merging.

**Aliases:** The Ivory Gate Syndicate, The Pass-Masters, The Horn & Crag Fleet, The Heavy Vane.
**Constituent castes:** Ceratomorphs (Dust-Horns / Rhinoceros-kin) and Caprines (Crag-Hoppers / Goat-kin).
**Doctrine:** "The Sky Belongs to No Bank. Flat Fees, Iron Escorts, Free Skyways."
**Patron Dragon:** none — a purely mortal power, and that's fine as-is; not every faction needs one. Their identity comes from their own history, not a chaos-flavor influence.

**Origin:** Two once-unrelated peoples (Caprine goat-herders and Ceratomorph rhino-keepers) shared a quiet, informal symbiosis in the Ivory Pass — until Aether-Skiff technology turned their mountain pass into a commercial shortcut. Corporate crews and Corsair pirates treated the locals as beasts of burden, dumping waste and taking potshots for sport, until a crew desecrated a birthing ground and killed a Goat elder. The resulting war-party beat the offending crew half to death and dismantled their ship. When the locals tried to formalize a toll for safe passage, corporate/Sky-Baron fleets crushed the attempt by force — until the natives ambushed and captured an entire heavily escorted trade convoy in the pass's narrowest point, then built the **Bone-Toll Towers**, displaying the remains of anyone who ran the blockade as a warning.

**Today:** A globally deployed, heavily armed toll authority and mercenary fleet — not confined to their home canyon. Payment earns an **Ivory Chit** (carved from the bones of the first corporate fleets they destroyed) and peerless protected passage; refusal earns nothing but silence — "they don't kill you; they just refuse to anchor you. They let the sky have you."

**Business doctrine — the "Hostile Takeover" (salvaged from the retired Crimson Corsairs material):** their day-to-day enforcement runs on calculated, quasi-legal psychological warfare rather than pure brutality — a multi-stage interception process designed to make surrender feel legally and financially inevitable before a shot is fired. War Skiffs function as much like "floating legal offices backed by overwhelming firepower" as combat craft. This isn't a contradiction with the Bone-Toll Towers' brutal deterrence — it's the professionalized, day-to-day face of the same organization: warm the target up with contracts and statutes; only escalate to visible brutality (and the display of remains) when the paperwork fails.

**Related find — the Guerrilla Clans:** while tracing the Ghostwind Raiders' origin, found they're not their own nation but a cell of **the Guerrilla Clans** (small cat, rabbit, and rodent folk) — a distinct power from the Verdant Tangle/Canopy Clans, likely the western neighbor referenced for the Sylvan Empire above. Not yet given a full write-up; flagging as a 17th faction that exists in the source and needs one.
