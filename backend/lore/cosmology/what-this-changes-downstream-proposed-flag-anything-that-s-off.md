# What this changes downstream (proposed — flag anything that's off)
| Old framing (purge) | New framing |
|---|---|
| Tectonic plates literally rotate/torque 5–15° every Meander | The **leyline flow network** re-routes along the same 49-day cycle, tracking Cruorbus's orbital position — flow paths shift by a few degrees and expand/contract. Ground doesn't move; the danger zones do. |
| "Aetheric Drag" physically drags continents toward the World-Drain | Aetheric Drag is the pull exerted *within* an active flow — a real hazard to anything caught in one, especially near the Convergence, but not a force acting on the crust itself |
| "The Groan" = seismic resonance of grinding plates | The Groan = a low-frequency hum given off as the leyline network re-tensions during a flow-shift — audible/felt (this is what a Jitter-Rig actually detects) |
| Grind Canyons = rift zones torn open by plates grinding | Grind Canyons = real canyons, carved/eroded over centuries by a major leyline branch running unusually violent and corrosive through that terrain (this is also why Port Sunder is such a rich Dragonstone site — it sits where that flow runs heaviest) |
| "Hydrological Nightmare" — oceans draining into the mantle | **Cut entirely.** Not related to oceans in any way. |
| Dragonstone = "crystallized fragments of the Clockwork," found everywhere | Dragonstone = literal meteoric debris from **Erranith**, ejected during its 169-year close passes. Finite, falls in a specific event, then gets hunted/mined until the next storm. |
| Shadow Week tied to Erranith's precession, framed as "peak Blowout/reality-tearing violence" | See below — resolved. |
