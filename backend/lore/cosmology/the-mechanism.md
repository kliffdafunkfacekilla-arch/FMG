# The mechanism
- **The 12 Sacred Groves** (Dragon Prisons) sit at fixed points across the globe, spaced equidistant from one another in an **icosahedral (d20) pattern**. Each Grove actively pulls in and absorbs the local share of Cruorbus's constant bombardment — this absorbed energy is what keeps each prison's seal strong enough to hold its Magistar.
- Whatever energy a Grove doesn't need is **excess**, and it's channeled along **ancient leylines** toward a single central point: **the Convergence** — the site of the Weeping Wound, the same wound torn by the Fae Exodus.
- **The Convergence is a real drain — just not of the ocean.** It's a drain for chaos energy, pulling it back toward the void it originally leaked in from.
- At the Convergence, that energy is processed by the **Celestial Clockwork** into the raw material of the **12 Powers** — the actual substrate of magic and reality itself. Whatever isn't processed leaks back out through Cruorbus's bombardment and re-enters the cycle from the top.

**The whole flow-and-drain system — Cruorbus's bombardment, Grove absorption, leyline channeling, Convergence processing, recycling — is "the Patch."** The Celestial Clockwork broke; the Elder Gods' sacrifice and The Engineer's solo labor couldn't fix it, only rig this circulatory system to keep it running. It's not a dying machine falling apart — it's a broken machine on a working life-support loop, with one catastrophic 169-year maintenance spike (Erranith's pass) built in.
