# The 8 Seasons (corrected — mechanism reassigned from Erranith to Cruorbus)
The 8 Seasons aren't Erranith's tumble on a 49-day cycle after all — they're **Cruorbus's own libration, spanning a full year.** Cruorbus is tidally locked (always facing Ostraka), but it "shakes its head no" in a slow **Horizontal Wobble** that takes a full 6 months to complete one pass, while simultaneously tumbling **vertically** (a slower, complicating Precession). Together these produce **8 distinct positions** across a full year — two 6-month wobble-cycles, each with 4 phases:

**Cycle A (the Upper Wobble), first half of the year:**
1. The Leering — top-left damage
2. The Open Eye — face-on damage
3. The Receding — top-right damage
4. The Halo — face-away, the clean/calm phase

**Cycle B (the Lower Wobble), second half of the year:** the same four phases repeat from the opposite vertical position, giving 8 total named seasons across the year.

The underlying logic is the same as before, just correctly attributed: when Cruorbus's stained, chaos-leaking face points toward the surface, the leyline network runs hotter; face-away phases (like the Halo) run comparatively calm. Which quadrant it's pointing from determines *where* on the globe that season hits hardest. **This is a separate, much slower rhythm from the 49-day Meander** (which is still the faster day-to-day flow-shift cycle) — think of the Meander as weather and the 8 Seasons as the actual seasons. The calendar year ends with the 13-day intercalary **Shadow Week** (Section 1, above) once both cycles complete.
