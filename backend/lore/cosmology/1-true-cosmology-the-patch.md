# 1. True Cosmology: The Patch
**Ostraka is a normal, solid globe.** It does not float in fragments, and no plate has ever torqued, pivoted, or drifted apart from another. What's actually unstable is the *energy* moving across and through it — the ground stays put; the danger moves.
