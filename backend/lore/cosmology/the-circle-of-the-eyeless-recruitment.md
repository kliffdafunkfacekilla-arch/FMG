# The Circle of the Eyeless — recruitment
*(Publicly, they're seen as wandering, gray-robed ascetics — hence "Grey Wardens" — masking the true organization beneath. Their unending clandestine war is specifically against the Worm Cults, their ideological opposite.)*

**A shared genesis with the Mandate of the Chain, confirmed:** the Grey Wardens and the Mandate of the Chain/Covenant of Null (Section 4.20) both trace back to the same original population — those who lived in the sacred land around the Engineer as his body petrified, imprinted with his final, fading duty: *"Guard the prisons."* That single shared origin split into two distinct, connected peoples: the Wardens, who kept the mission intact, and the ancestors of the Mandate, who eventually drifted, lost the full context, and rebuilt it into a misreading of their own inheritance. It's not two unrelated groups who happened to find the same lost documents — they're estranged family.

**The Crucible of the Initiate ("The Blind"):** recruitment deliberately seeks out the broken — recruits are often criminals, slaves, or orphans, put through brutal hard labor and sleep deprivation to scour away their old identity before being reforged as loyal Wardens. The Circle of the Eyeless specifically are the oldest and wisest active Wardens, who undergo a ritual burning out their own eyes to replace them with Chaos Sight, letting them see anywhere, anytime, by "tricking" chaos's own erratic nature.

**Rank progression, in full (the fuller, more detailed version — supersedes the earlier "Four Core Branches" simplification):** a new recruit starts as raw labor, watched by a mentoring "shepherd" until deemed ready to join **a Claw** — a twelve-warden patrol unit. Surviving a first patrol, and being ritually accepted by that Claw, promotes them to **Initiate** — training with a chosen weapon and their innate power(s), and naturally settling into one of **seven wings**: the Arms, the Eyes, the Mind, the Body/Heart, the Mouth, the Legs, or the Tail. After 12 years of service, Initiates face **the first sealed vault's trial** (unlocked once initiates show they understand and can knowingly channel the Twelve Powers) — a ritual of ego-stripping that teaches sigil-based chaos channeling; only **1 in 3 survive**, and those who do become **Acolytes**. Some Acolytes are eventually drawn to discover a hidden chamber (**the second sealed vault**, unlocked once several Acolytes demonstrate deep mastery and wisdom about the Celestial Clockwork and chaos balance) — undergoing **the Rite of Attunement**, becoming the living embodiment of a weapon, granting the rank of **Master** (an unnerving stillness, always armed even mid-conversation). A few Masters are drawn again, via a secret staircase, to a mountain-top crater for **the Eyeless ritual** — sometimes lasting weeks; only **1 in 10** survive both this and the earlier vault trial, emerging as **the Eyeless**, all-seeing demigods who lead the Wardens. *(Note some naming overlap in the source between "Master"/"Master of Arms" and "Seer"/"Grand Justicar" for adjacent ranks — reads like the original author's own working draft rather than a fully reconciled hierarchy; flagging rather than forcing a clean fix.)*

**The Prophet's Silent Bell network, expanded:** not just a recruitment trigger — a Sparkborn who crosses one experiences a harsh, forced vision of the true horror of the prisons and the chaos flow. The Prophet paired these with a hidden network of warded waypoints (outcroppings, tree copses, stream crossings) shielding travelers on the road to the Spire — most now forgotten, some destroyed, many still active and still recharging on their own schedule after each trigger, which is why their locations stay unnoticed. Local folklore about "cursed" or "haunted" places is sometimes an accurate folk-memory of a real ward. **Confirmed again, directly:** one such sanctuary was exposed in the past, and the fragmented information recovered from it is what led to the Mandate of the Chain's founding misreading (Section 4.20).

**The Mage's other legacy:** beyond founding the Circle of the Eyeless and the precedent of Aetheric Sacrifice (giving one's life to stabilize a Chaos Zone), Valen also founded **the school of sigil threading** — initiates learning to bind elemental threads and tattoos to channel chaos into a controllable form. This is the direct origin of the Elemental Conductor/sigil system described throughout Section 2 and Section 5 — one unified magic tradition, not a separate invention.
