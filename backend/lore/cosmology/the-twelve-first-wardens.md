# The Twelve First Wardens
Not Magistars, and not simple defectors from Dominion tyranny — **the Twelve First Wardens were captains serving in the Magistars' own armies**, and their defection happened *during* the Divine War itself. It was these twelve who exposed Sh'lar's trickery to the Elder Gods (who'd initially been fooled into fighting each other), redirecting the war toward its real target. **They were the last surviving humans on Ostraka.** Each had their own breaking point — the specific moment their loyalty to their Magistar snapped:

| Warden | Former Master | Role Before Defection | The Breaking Point |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kaelen the Unmovable | Tiraton (Mass) | High-Executioner | Ordered to collapse a mountain onto fleeing civilians for a clear artillery line; stood in the stone's path and held it up until everyone escaped. |
| Jiro the Resolute | Carulkem (Nexus) | Chief Inquisitor | Shown a vision of the world's beauty while torturing a Fae prisoner; released every prisoner instead. |
| Myra of the Aegis | Stagus (Ordo) | Personal Bodyguard | Ordered to drain an entire orphanage's life-force for one spell; turned her shield inward and broke the ritual. |
| Boros the Forgetter | Tyrustis (Lex) | Labor Enforcer | Ordered to kill his own father for "inefficiency"; shielded him instead and led a mass exodus into the tunnels. |
| Ren the Instinct | Vecelo (Motus) | Strike Team Leader | Ordered to slaughter his own squad as a speed test; disarmed the Magistar instead. |
| Sylas the Swift | Aurgenas (Flux) | Spymaster | Discovered the plot to sacrifice the Fae; spent decades smuggling Fae families out before defecting fully. |
| Elara the Pure | Gavusrix (Vita) | Blood-Alchemist | Tasked with harvesting Fae essence for Gavusrix's longevity; secretly used it to create an anti-virus protecting the rebel camps instead. |
| Thrum the Mason | Metrion (Ratio) | High-Architect | Ordered to design a city that doubled as an execution chamber; built in a hidden flaw that collapsed it on the Magistar's own legions. |
| Valen the Witness | Opecten (Lux) | Surveillance Officer | Watched a child share their last food with a stranger; blinded Opecten's sensors to let the rebel armies assemble in secret. |
| Fane the Gambler | Termhill (Omen) | Chief Diviner | Foresaw the Shattering; Termhill laughed at the warning, so he stole Dragonstone and bet everything on defecting. |
| Liora the Truth-Bearer | Virantor (Aura) | Chief Propagandist | The voice that convinced the masses slavery was divine service; one day spoke the truth into the amplifiers instead, sparking the riot that gave the rebellion its opening. |
| Sora the Shielded | Lophex (Anumis) | Librarian of the Vault | Realized Lophex's "knowledge" was a blueprint for annihilation; memorized the counter-sequence, burned the archives, and fled with the only cure. |
