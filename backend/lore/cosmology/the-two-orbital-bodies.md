# The two orbital bodies
- **Erranith** is the Sun God's eye, and **Cruorbus** ("The Blood Orphan") is his discarded remains — both explained fully in the origin story above.
- **The Magistars pulled Cruorbus into orbit** during their ascension ritual — that working reached out and hauled Cruorbus's drifting corpse into a close orbit around Ostraka. Cruorbus settling into that close orbit is what knocked Erranith into its long, erratic, wide-swinging path instead — Erranith is the one that's shattered, and *why* it shattered has its own real mythic answer too: the Wolves (Section 4, Heartland Alliance) hold that the Moon broke because they looked away from it for one moment, and their entire warrior culture is built on doing eternal penance for that failure.
- **Cruorbus provides the constant background bombardment.** Its steady presence is what continuously rains ambient chaos energy onto Ostraka, feeding the Groves and driving the regular 49-day Meander cycle as its position shifts.
- **Erranith provides the rare catastrophic event.** Roughly every **169 Ostraka years**, its erratic orbit swings it back into close proximity, triggering wild chaos flux worldwide and a massive meteor storm. **That meteor debris — fragments of shattered Erranith — is the literal source of Dragonstone.** It's not "crystallized everywhere"; it's a finite resource that rains down once every 169 years and gets mined/hoarded until the next storm.
