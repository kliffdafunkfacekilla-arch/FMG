# The Jitter-Rig
The primary navigational tool: a raw, unsigiled Dragonstone shard suspended in a tuning-fork framework (iron, copper, quartz). Navigators monitor the forks' resonance to detect **the Groan** before it's felt anywhere else, adjusting course ahead of a flow-shift. *(Corrected: source material universally describes this as detecting "tectonic plates torquing during a rotational shift" — per Section 1, the Jitter-Rig actually detects the leyline network re-tensioning as chaos flow paths shift course, not the ground moving. The instrument, its use, and its importance to navigation are otherwise unchanged — only the cause it's sensing needs updating.)*

**During Shadow Week specifically:** Navigators must manually "adapt the flow" through the rig to prevent an engine desync, since ambient chaos behaves differently while the network is slowed by the eclipse. This still works under the new model — the specific danger is different (a slowed, calming flow rather than a "violently tearing" one) but the Jitter-Rig's job — keeping the ship's systems synced to whatever the ambient flow is actually doing — doesn't change.
