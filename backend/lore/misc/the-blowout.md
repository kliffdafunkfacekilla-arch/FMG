# The Blowout
A **Blowout** is a catastrophic containment failure — not an explosion, an *unmaking*. Consequences include instantaneous biological petrification, localized gravity reversals, or matter simply ceasing to be. This is consistent everywhere it appears — no correction needed, it already matches the corrected cosmology (a Blowout is what happens when a Grove's seal or a piece of Aether-Tech fails and lets raw, unrouted chaos flow through unfiltered).
