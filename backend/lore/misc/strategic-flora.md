# Strategic Flora
A "Botanical Codex" dossier catalogs five plants with real geopolitical weight. Two are fully described in the source; three exist only as a name and one-word subtitle. Fleshed out below — clearly marked which parts are sourced versus extrapolated from the world's existing logic.

| Plant | Subtitle | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **Grave-Root** | The Predator of the Ambush | *(Sourced.)* Carnivorous flora that mimics stone or dead timber; uses root-based vibration sensing to strike ground-level prey, while avoiding high-risk Flux-Shift zones itself. |
| **Stone-Root** | Petrified Lumber | *(Sourced.)* Fossilized mineral-plant, essential for heavy construction and firearm furniture — its high mineral density makes it entirely fireproof. |
| **Lith-Weave** | The Stone-Silk | *(Sourced, connection found elsewhere in the corpus.)* This is the actual material powering the Meridian Chain's **Ghost-Light Towers** — the lighthouses that project Anti-Chaos Fields, carving safe shipping lanes and protecting Bridge-Cities from time-warps. A mineral-fiber "stone-silk" strong and stable enough to be woven into the towers' structure and used as a conduit for holding an Anti-Chaos Field steady. Managed by the Order of the Wick's monastic Eels, who harvest raw material from a Sacred Grove itself to power it — meaning Lith-Weave is likely a plant that grows specifically in the high-chaos margins around a Grove, its structure toughened by proximity the same way Grave-Root and Stone-Root are shaped by their own dangerous environments. |
| **Crimson-Clove** | Numb-Spice | *Sourced: scientific name Syzygium Anestheticum, confirming the numbing/anesthetic property directly. Cultivated by the Vulpines (Foxes) in the Scent-Gardens, a mid-canopy zone.* Function beyond that is still extrapolated — my read remains that it's the medicinal agent used by **Ink-Wrights during the Shadow-Set ritual** (Section 4.1), since nothing else in the corpus explains how anyone survives that "high-risk, surgical" tattoo rite conscious. |
| **Whisper-Berry** | Sparkle-Fruit | *Sourced: scientific name Vitis Aeris ("air-vine"), cultivated by the Simians (Monkeys) in the Wind-Tunnels, another mid-canopy zone.* Function beyond that is still extrapolated — my read remains that it's a chaos-sensitive plant that glimmers with intensity near a flow-shift, doubling as a natural early-warning system and a sharpening aid for the empathic **Whisperer** role (Section 5). |

**Ghost Flowers** — not in the five-plant Codex list but extensively used elsewhere and worth including here: essential Aether-engine stabilizers, a high-value/high-risk smuggled good moved by the Sump-Kin's Silent Current and the Heartland Alliance's River Ghosts. Must be transported inside the water-rich internal cavities of Cactus-Kin — exposure to open air ("Dry Rot") causes the flower to petrify into uselessness.

**Ghost Flowers = Lunar Ghost-Blooms, confirmed.** Biological "Chaos-Sponges" that absorb ambient Moon-Haze (chaos-bleed) rather than concentrated flow, growing forever but capping at 3 meters — older growth is denser and more valuable. Burning a field doesn't just smoke, it releases a mutation cloud. Harvested by the **Dust-Husk Riders** (an organization not yet fully cataloged). Three derivative industries: **Aether-Clear** (the Porcupines'/Quill-Kin's real medicinal export, Section 4.9 — now confirmed sourced from this plant), **Void-Cream** (cosmetics), and **"The 4-Year Seed"** (a narcotic).
