# 7. Timeline
Cross-checked against every faction founding story with a dated event — **everything lines up cleanly, no contradictions found.**

**Pre-history (a nice extra layer, found while checking dates):**
- **Era B.S. ("Before Shattering")** — ends ~6650 B.S.
- **Era S.S. ("The Shattered Sky")** — ~6650 to 3000 B.S.
- *(Note: "Shattering" here reads as the general term for the Fae Exodus/Magistar-transformation cataclysm from Section 1, not literal ground-fragmentation — worth keeping the name since it's evocative and doesn't actually conflict with the corrected cosmology once understood as a name for the cosmic event rather than a physical description of the planet.)*

**On the First Wardens:** see Section 1 for the full, current account — they were captains in the Magistars' own armies who defected during the Divine War, not defectors from the Magistars' ascension itself.

**The A.W. ("After Wardens") era, confirmed structure:**
| Era | Years (A.W.) | Key dated events found, cross-checked |
|---|---|---|
| I | 0–150 | — |
| II: The Feral Epoch | 151–500 | The Schism of the Scar (~250 A.W., splits Sylvan Empire/Flower Valley Folk); the Delta Wars (~400 A.W., founds the River Folk/Sump-Kin split) |
| III: The Era of the Widening Eye | 501–800 | The Webweaver Wars & Gruesome Accords (151–~700 A.W., founds Vaneer Concord governance — spans into Era II, which is fine, wars don't respect era boundaries) |
| IV: The Age of First Contact | 801–1499 | The Magistar engine core explosion at Shadow Week onset (~1000 A.W., founds the Avian Doctrine of the Mint); the Opal-Wallow Summit (~1080 A.W., founds the Fulcrum Bank Proxy System) |
| V: The Combustion of the Skies | 1500–1850 | The Aether-Skiff is invented in exactly 1500 A.W. — the event the era is named for, and the trigger for the Ivory-Gate Syndicate's founding conflict, the Sylvan/Flower Valley pattern of sky-lane invasions, and most airship-era faction origins |
| VI: The Modern Gridlock | 1851–2000 | The Guerrilla Clans/Sky-Baron violation (1892 A.W.); Mama Koda's pirate rise (1985 A.W.) and confirmed exile (1960 A.W.) |

**Present day, inferred:** never stated as a hard number in the updated timeline, but Mama Koda's exile was explicitly re-dated to 1960 A.W. specifically "to keep her an active, living threat in the present day" — placing the current setting somewhere in the back half of Era VI, likely close to but before 2000 A.W.

**One superseded draft, already flagged by the source itself:** an earlier version of this same timeline used different numbers entirely (Last Reckoning 1590 A.W., Koda's exile 1620 A.W., "current year" 1650 A.W.) — the source explicitly marks the 6-era/1960 A.W. version as the more recently updated one, so that's canon; the old numbers can be discarded.

**No faction founding dates without their own contradictions found** — the Scute Confederacy, Iron Caldera, and Ivory-Gate Syndicate all have origin stories but no fixed year attached to them in the source, which isn't a conflict, just undated. Fine to leave undated or assign a year yourself if you want them pinned down.
