# 4.24 Notable Figures

The 12 First Wardens now have their own dedicated table in Section 1. Remaining named individuals compiled below as a reference table rather than full bios, to keep this document usable — flag any for a full write-up if they matter to what you're working on.

| Figure | Role | Key detail |
|---|---|---|
| **Mama Koda** ("The Ice-Branded") | Hearthless Queen of the Undine, ruler of the casino/black-market hub *Undine's Undying Dance* | Real name Koda of the Snowpeak; was a decorated Captain in the **Wolverine Brotherhood** (an independent, apolitical martial order guarding both Ursine Hegemony and Heartland Alliance) until Shadow Week 1960 A.W. (Section 7), when she broke protocol to open the gates for a freezing caravan of "Drifter" refugees — court-martialed, branded with the molten Sigil of Ice, and cast into the blizzard. Walked south to the River Delta, took up smuggling muscle work on an **Ivory-Gate Syndicate** barge (retconned from "Crimson Corsairs" — Section 4.15), and eventually won *Undine's Undying Dance* — then a rotting "Scab" of a tavern — from its slumlord owner Felix the Rot in a game of Dead Man's Poker (1985 A.W.). Poured her pirate fortune into rebuilding it into the continent's premier casino, employing an impeccably dressed Sciurini steward, **Daniaz**, to handle high-society diplomacy she has no patience for herself. Wears River Folk finery under a mocking fur cloak; the Sigil of Ice brand is never hidden. |
| **Emperor Aurelius III** ("The Prism"/"The White Peacock") | Current Avian Empire emperor | Rules through "Aesthetic Domination" rather than his predecessors' evasion or military might — believes a flawed-looking Empire is a flawed Empire. |
| **Emperor Kaelos** ("The Evasive") | Avian Empire's cultural saint (historical) | Author of *The Book of Omissions*, the foundational text on navigating the Avian inability to lie — his own trial-by-interrogation is the legend every Avian fledgling learns. |
| **Grand Auditor Keth** ("The Ink-Blood") | High Director, Fulcrum Bank | Ancient Great Horned Owl, 60-year tenure; secretly the current bearer of "the First Coin" (the Director's Secret). |
| **High-Admiral Kaelen** ("The Golden Talon") | Supreme Commander, Imperial Fleet | Captains the flagship *Radiant Raptor*; signature tactic is unnerving silence. |
| **Captain Vira** ("The Dive-Bomber") | Captain, *The Felicitous Falcon* | The Cloud-Harriers' daring "Ace" rather than a Roost politician — the only Imperial figure the Free Sky-Baronies actually respect. |
| **Trade-Master Hrothe** ("The Carrion King") | Captain, *The Consummate Condor* | Runs "Carrion Economics" — buys up what other factions have broken. |
| **Sh'lar** | The 13th Shadow, Prime Corruptor | Full alias set confirmed: The Whisper in the Static, The Rot, The Ghost in the Gear. Chose to dissolve into the chaos willingly during the Shattering rather than being transformed against her will — now a diffused, non-corporeal intelligence permeating every Chaos Zone. |
| **Captain Jax** | Pilot, the Write Wager | Catfish-Kin; a "P"-shaped brass jaw-plate scar from an unexplained youth incident; keeps hard candy for children he meets. Likely a founding Order of the Clockwork member. |
| **"Songbird" (Hazard A Chance)** | Navigator, the Write Wager | Disgraced Red-winged Blackbird Avian, ritually clipped flightless, formerly Compass Navy (Avian military) — wears his old uniform unbuttoned as a "Handsome Rogue" affectation. |
| **Pipin Pringle** ("Pringle") | Cook, the Write Wager | Twelve-segmented Caterpillar-Kin; walks walls/ceilings via prolegs; can "produce" blades from his body segments and spin adhesive silk. |
| **Kog Viomuth** | Engineer, the Write Wager | Monitor Lizard-Kin; a blind, heat-vision-only left eye from industrial scarring; custom Forge Goggles with independently flippable lenses. |
| **Celandine "Leaf" Valeroot** | The Whisperer (Section 5) | Flower Valley Folk; alabaster birch-bark skin; fell from high-canopy estate poise to "the Gutter" via a personal betrayal that struck at her identity as an information broker. |
| **Clary, The First Archivist** | Founder, Order of the Clockwork (Section 4.22) | Aliases: The Performance-Scholar, The Weaver of Truths. Origin village destroyed not by war but by a Chaos Zone during a violent Shadow Week — became a refugee before founding the Order. |
