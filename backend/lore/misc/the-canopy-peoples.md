# The Canopy Peoples
A structured "Profile" documentation project covers the mid-canopy species who cultivate the Strategic Flora above and staff the trade/craft professions tied to them — mostly headers with sparse body text in the source, same pattern as elsewhere. Compiled as a quick reference:
