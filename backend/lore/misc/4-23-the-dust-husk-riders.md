# 4.23 The Dust-Husk Riders

**Not a species — a symbiotic fleet.** Nomads of the Dust Bowl, a region so hostile it's a "No-Fly Zone" for every other faction — a confederation of radically different plant-like peoples, each contributing a specialized biological role to the fleet rather than one uniform culture. This diversity is their actual strategic advantage: no single species could perceive and survive the Dust Bowl alone. They're the primary harvesters of **Lunar Ghost-Blooms** (Section 6), the source of Aether-Clear, Void-Cream, and the narcotic "4-Year Seed."
